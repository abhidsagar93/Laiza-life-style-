    Our Story

    The Laiza Story

    Laiza began in Sagar, Karnataka — a small town, a bigger idea. We wanted to bring genuine, full-grain leather goods to people who had only ever been offered synthetic imitations dressed up as "premium."

    The name Laiza carries our mark — the rising flame you see on every piece — a symbol of presence, quality, and quiet confidence. You'll find it on every Laiza wallet we sell.

    Today, Laiza is launching its first collection: a line of wallets and card holders designed to be felt, used, and trusted for years — not seasons.

    "Real leather tells a story that lasts a lifetime — and every Laiza piece is meant to tell yours."

    Good to Know

    Policies

    We ship across India via trusted courier partners. Orders are processed and dispatched within 1–2 business days, with delivery typically taking 5–7 business days from dispatch. You'll receive tracking details once your order ships.

    Eligible returns or exchanges must be requested within 7 days of delivery, with the product unused and in its original packaging. Damaged, defective, or incorrect items should be reported within 48 hours. Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days.

    Orders can be cancelled free of charge any time before dispatch — contact us with your order number to cancel. Once an order has shipped, it can no longer be cancelled, but it may be eligible for return after delivery.

    We collect only the details needed to process your order — name, address, contact number, and payment details. We never sell your personal information, and share it only with delivery and payment partners as needed to fulfil your order.

    By ordering from LAIZA, you confirm the information you provide is accurate and agree to our order, pricing, and delivery terms. Product images are representative, and prices are listed in Indian Rupees and subject to change.
